How to use

For face and neck: Apply 2–3 drops to clean, slightly damp skin. Gently massage until absorbed.

For hands, cuticles and body: Apply a few drops to desired areas and massage gently, adding more as needed.

When to use: Morning and evening, or whenever skin needs extra moisturizing care.

Care: For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs.
