How to use

Face & neck: Apply 2–3 drops to clean, slightly damp skin. Gently massage until absorbed.

Hands & cuticles: Massage a small amount over hands and around cuticles to soften dry-feeling areas.

Body: Smooth a few drops over dry skin wherever additional moisturizing care is needed.

Daily routine: Use morning and evening, or as needed for extra moisture and nourishment.

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Nature’s golden secret for beautifully radiant skin

Discover the luxurious power of EMEL Organic Sea Buckthorn Oil, a premium, 100% pure, cold-pressed botanical oil formulated by nature to nourish, moisturize and soften dry-looking skin.

Derived from the remarkable sea buckthorn plant (Hippophae rhamnoides), this exceptional oil is valued for its naturally occurring fatty acids, vitamin E compounds and nourishing botanical lipids.

For centuries, sea buckthorn has been appreciated as a valuable botanical resource. Today its oil is a sought-after ingredient in premium skincare, offering natural conditioning and moisturizing properties.

Whether you’re looking to replenish moisture, improve skin softness or enhance your natural glow, EMEL Sea Buckthorn Oil makes a beautiful addition to your daily skincare routine.

Naturally occurring omega fatty acids: Sea buckthorn oil contains omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids that help maintain the skin’s moisture balance. These botanical lipids contribute to softer, smoother and more supple-feeling skin.

Vitamin E – natural antioxidant nourishment: Naturally occurring tocopherols complement the oil’s conditioning properties and support a healthy-looking, radiant complexion.

Moisturizing care for dry, rough-looking skin: Helps soften rough areas, replenish moisture and promote a comfortable, well-conditioned skin feel.

Beautifully radiant appearance: Consistent moisture care helps soften the look of dryness-related fine lines and uneven skin texture, leaving the complexion looking refreshed and radiant.
