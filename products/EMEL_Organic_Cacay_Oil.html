Daily routine: Use morning and evening, or whenever your skin needs extra moisture.

Nature’s golden secret for radiant, youthful-looking skin

Discover the luxurious power of nature with EMEL Premium Organic Cacay Oil, a 100% pure, cold-pressed botanical oil designed to nourish, moisturize and revitalize dry-looking skin. Extracted from the seeds of the Amazonian Cacay tree (Caryodendron orinocense), it provides essential fatty acids, naturally occurring vitamin E compounds and skin-conditioning botanical nutrients.

Often called “Amazonian Gold,” Cacay Oil is valued for its lightweight texture and moisturizing properties. It helps skin feel silky-soft without a heavy, greasy residue.

Naturally rich in omega-6 linoleic acid: Cacay Oil is particularly rich in linoleic acid, an essential fatty acid that supports the skin’s natural moisture barrier, helps maintain moisture and promotes a softer, smoother, more supple complexion.

Vitamin E – natural antioxidant nourishment: Naturally occurring vitamin E compounds, including tocopherols, contribute to the oil’s antioxidant profile and complement its skin-conditioning properties.

Moisturizing care for fine lines and uneven texture: Dryness can make fine lines and skin texture more noticeable. Consistent use helps soften dry areas and improve the appearance of dryness-related fine lines, for a smoother-looking complexion.

Lightweight, fast-absorbing feel: Cacay Oil provides a silky finish and comfortable daily moisturizing care without an overly oily sensation.
