How to use

Morning and/or evening: Dispense one pump into your palms and gently press onto clean, damp skin.

Application: Massage gently over the face, neck and décolleté until evenly distributed. Repeat if additional moisture care is desired.

Daytime: Finish with broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen.

Caution: For external use only. Avoid direct contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs.

The beauty of Camellia & Cacay

Japanese Camellia Japonica Seed Oil (Tsubaki): Traditionally valued in Japanese-inspired beauty rituals, Camellia Seed Oil is naturally rich in nourishing fatty acids, particularly oleic acid. It provides emollient care that helps soften and condition the skin, leaving it feeling silky, smooth and comfortable.

Cacay Seed Oil: Extracted from the seeds of the Cacay tree (Caryodendron orinocense), this distinctive botanical oil contains omega-6 linoleic acid and naturally occurring antioxidant compounds. Cacay Oil helps nourish dry-feeling skin and support a softer, smoother-looking complexion. Its emollient properties make it a valuable ingredient in botanical skincare focused on maintaining moisture, suppleness and radiance.

Vitamin E (Tocopherol): An antioxidant ingredient that helps protect the botanical oils in the formula against oxidation and contributes to the skin-conditioning properties of the blend.
