How to use

Face, neck & décolleté: Press and massage gently until the facial oil is evenly distributed.

Daytime protection: Finish with broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen.

  • Bakuchiol Anti-Aging Facial Serum: Features bakuchiol, a botanical ingredient studied as an alternative to retinol for improving the appearance of fine lines, uneven texture and dull-looking skin. Helps maintain a smoother, youthful-looking complexion as part of your daily skincare routine.
  • Lightweight Squalane Moisturizer: Squalane and meadowfoam seed oil deliver silky, lightweight hydration that helps soften dry, rough skin, reduce moisture loss and promote a naturally plump, glowing complexion.   • For Normal, Dry and Mature Skin: A nourishing facial oil booster for normal, dry, mature and dull-looking complexions. Apply one pump to clean, damp skin morning or evening, gently pressing onto the face, neck and décolleté. 30 mL (1 fl oz) pump bottle.

Why your skin will love it

  • Bakuchiol — A Botanical Retinol Alternative: Bakuchiol is a plant-derived cosmetic ingredient researched for its potential to improve the appearance of fine lines, uneven skin texture and other visible signs of aging.
  • Squalane — Lightweight Moisture Care: Squalane helps soften and condition the skin while reducing moisture loss. Its lightweight emollient texture makes it a valuable ingredient in daily facial oil treatments.
  • Japanese Camellia (Tsubaki) Seed Oil: Traditionally valued in Japanese-inspired beauty rituals, Camellia Japonica Seed Oil contains nourishing fatty acids that help leave skin feeling smooth and supple.
  • Meadowfoam and Moringa Seed Oils: These botanical oils provide additional emollient care to maintain a soft, conditioned appearance without the feel of a heavy cream.
  • Black Currant, Cranberry and Pomegranate Seed Oils: A complementary blend of botanical seed oils containing naturally occurring fatty acids and antioxidant compounds.
  • Papaya Seed Oil and Vitamin E: Papaya Seed Oil contributes to the nourishing botanical blend, while tocopherol provides antioxidant activity to help maintain the quality of the oil formula.
