How to use

For scalp massage: Apply a small amount to fingertips and gently massage the scalp for 2–3 minutes. Avoid pulling or rubbing aggressively.

For hair conditioning: Apply a few drops to the mid-lengths and ends of dry or damp hair. Distribute evenly, focusing on dry-feeling areas.

For high shine & frizz control: Smooth a tiny amount over finished hair to enhance gloss and tame the appearance of flyaways. Start with a small amount and add more only if needed.

For pre-wash treatment: Massage a small amount through hair lengths and ends before washing for additional conditioning care. Use sparingly.

Lightweight botanical smoothing & shine serum

Discover silky, glossy-looking hair with EMEL Camellia High Shine Hair Oil, a luxurious, silicone-free botanical treatment designed to soften dry hair, smooth the appearance of frizz and enhance natural shine.

This lightweight oil serum combines Japanese Camellia Seed Oil, plant-derived Squalane, Rice Bran Oil and Vitamin E for smooth, manageable, beautifully conditioned hair.

The beauty of four ingredients: Japanese Camellia (Tsubaki) for softness and shine; Squalane for lightweight slip; Rice Bran Oil for conditioning and frizz-smoothing; Vitamin E to help protect the oil blend against oxidation.

Ideal for: dry and frizzy hair, dull strands lacking shine, rough-feeling ends, flyaways and hair needing extra conditioning. Suitable for straight, wavy, curly and color-treated hair.

Key ingredients
